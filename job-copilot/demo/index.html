How it stays truthful: every tailored bullet must match one of your original bullets (80% word overlap or more), may not add any number, and may not claim more than the original: no upgrading "supported" to "led", no added words like "entire" or "single-handedly". Every claimed skill needs evidence in your resume. The cover letter is written only from approved bullets and checked too: every number must come from your resume, "led" or "owned" only where your matching bullet says so, and no skill gap claimed as experience. After that, an AI reviewer reads each bullet next to its original and flags subtler exaggeration for you to double-check; it never blocks, because AI reviewers make false alarms. If the Critic rejects a draft, the Tailor gets one retry with the Critic's notes; if it still fails, the draft is withheld and sent for human review.

Live mode sends your text to Claude using your own Claude account and asks your permission first. Phone numbers and emails are removed before anything is sent.
